FINAL RESEARCH FINDINGS
======================================================================

RQ1 — CLASS IMBALANCE
The effect of imbalance handling was model-dependent. The original-data XGBoost configuration achieved the strongest ranking performance, while class weighting and resampling generally increased churn recall but did not necessarily improve Average Precision.

RQ2 — THRESHOLD AND DECISION COST
Lowering the decision threshold from 0.50 to the development-selected robust threshold of 0.18 substantially increased churn recall and reduced false negatives, but also increased false positives. The selected threshold reduced decision cost under the 5:1 and 10:1 FN-to-FP scenarios but was less favorable under the 2:1 scenario.

RQ3 — EXPLAINABILITY
SHAP analysis identified Contract, tenure, InternetService, MonthlyCharges, and OnlineSecurity as the five strongest original-feature contributors to the selected XGBoost model.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION NOTE
SHAP values describe model-level prediction contributions and associations, not causal effects.
